Tactile Permanence Over Seasonal Excess

At ALURA, garments are designed as wearable architecture. We bypass the rapid obsolescence of trend cycles, developing each piece across twenty-four months of fitting and fabric evolution.

Every garment begins with yarn selection from historic spinning mills in Biella and Kinross. By eliminating exterior hardware and unnecessary ornamentation, the silhouette speaks through density, drape, and the intimate hand of raw noble fibers.

01 / PROVENANCE

The Fiber Archive

We source exclusively from heritage European mills: Scottish 5-gauge cashmere, Giza 87 Egyptian cotton, and Tuscan vegetable-tanned full-grain leather.

02 / STRUCTURE

Unlined Lightness

By stripping out stiff adhesives and synthetic interlinings, our garments conform naturally to the body, holding shape through precise textile tension.

03 / GUARANTEE

Lifelong Repair

Every ALURA coat and tailored piece includes complimentary restorative mending at our Paris atelier, ensuring each edition endures for generations.
